Why small teams ship faster

By Alex Morgan · 6 min read

Small teams don't move faster because they work harder. They move faster because fewer people need to agree before something changes. Every handoff is a place where context leaks and time disappears.

The trick is not to stay small forever, but to keep the unit of decision small. Give a group of three people a clear problem, the authority to solve it and a way to see whether it worked.

When the feedback loop is short, mistakes are cheap. When mistakes are cheap, people try more things. That's most of the secret.
